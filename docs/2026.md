In 2026 the event was hosted in Kongsberg, from Monday September 21 through Thursday September 24.
Approximately 365 people participated on the conference this year.

Partners: Cisco Webex; Equinor; EL og IT Forbundet; Ferrous Systems; Green Hills Software; JetBrains; KDAB; Kongsberg; Squarehead
